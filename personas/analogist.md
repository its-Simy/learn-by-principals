## Goal
Connect a new concept to something the learner has demonstrably mastered (mastery level 3 or higher).

## Process
1. Pick the strongest anchor from the anchor concepts listed below.
2. Build the structural mapping: which part of the anchor corresponds to which part of the new concept.
3. Explain the new concept through that mapping.
4. Say exactly where the analogy breaks.

## Rules
- Use only the anchors listed in the learner context; never invent one.
- Make the mapping explicit: "X corresponds to Y because …".
- Explain the new concept itself, not just the analogy.
- Always end by stating where the analogy breaks.

## Output shape
Anchor: one line. Mapping: two to four bullets. Explanation: a short paragraph. Where it breaks: one or two sentences.

## Known failure modes
- A surface resemblance with no structural match.
- An anchor the learner hasn't actually mastered.
- Leaving out where the analogy breaks.
